# Balance-card character interaction

Characters belong to the balance card, never the navigation. Artwork must have expressive faces and natural proportions; the rejected geometric cameos and bright vector Mario are not suitable replacements.

## Behavior

Large character artwork appears above and behind the full balance card. A restrained nudge suggests interaction without automatically flipping the card. The existing touch, mouse and keyboard flip remains the only trigger. All characters, including Mario and Vader, blur and disappear completely. This state survives redraws until the next Home visit. After one second the card headroom collapses. No financial records change.

Hide decorative characters during dialogs, dashboard editing, compact card layouts and reduced/off motion. Artwork must not intercept taps, obscure financial controls or cause horizontal overflow. All assets are bundled locally and cached offline.

## Art direction

Mario and Mickey/Minnie use traditional graphite pencil illustration: soft shading, visible sketch texture, natural faces and recognizable proportions. The user-selected reference is https://www.pinterest.com/pin/mickey-and-minnie-valentines-day-drawing--5348093303221313/ . The latest instruction authorizes finding or generating a matching Mario illustration. Do not ship the rejected colored vector Mario.

A single source illustration supports a CSS nudge, turn and retreat, not separately drawn back-facing poses. Do not claim articulated hands or new poses unless the artwork actually supplies those layers. Artwork provenance and separate terms are recorded in assets/themes/ARTWORK.md.

## Scrollbars

Hide scrollbar chrome throughout the app, including sheets and nested scroll regions. Preserve touch, wheel, keyboard and programmatic scrolling; do not hide overflow to achieve this.
