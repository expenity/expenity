# Reports

Reports turn your transactions into charts and tables. Open them with the chart icon in the top bar of Home, Activity, Bills and Plan, or from **More**. The **All** view shows every report one after the other, each with an **Open** link, so you can scroll through everything. The buttons under the filters (each with an icon) open one report on its own. Pick a **period** at the top (This month, Last month, 3 months, This year, 12 months, or a custom range), then a tab. **Filters** narrow everything to a wallet, a category, only expenses or only income, or a word in the description. Goal money is left out of spending.

![The Overview tab.](images/reports-overview.png)

## Overview

Net worth, net result, income, expenses, savings rate and spending per day, each with an arrow and a percentage compared with the previous period of the same length. Below, the top categories and your biggest expense.

> 💡 **Real-life example** At the end of the month Nikos opens Reports and sees income, expenses, his savings rate and what he spent per day, all on one screen.

## Spending

A donut chart by category. Tap a slice or a legend row to drill down into that category's transactions. Also: average spending by weekday, where the money goes (by description) and the biggest expenses.

![Spending by category.](images/reports-spending.png)

> 💡 **Real-life example** Anna taps the biggest slice of the donut and sees the entries behind it: three restaurant bills explain most of it.

## Cash flow

Income and expenses month by month, cumulative cash flow, and your savings rate by month.

## Trends

Spending by month stacked by category (the top five and the rest), and how this month compares with last month.

> 💡 **Real-life example** Petros sees that his coffee spending has grown every month and decides to cut down.

## Forecast

The expected balance in 30, 60 and 90 days, drawn as a line. It uses your bills, your recurring income and a typical daily spend worked out from the last 60 days, and says so under the chart. It also shows **the amount you can spend per day** until the end of the month, the lowest point your balance reaches and the biggest payments coming up.

![The forecast.](images/reports-forecast.png)

> 💡 **Real-life example** Before a big purchase, Elena checks the forecast, which includes her upcoming bills and expected income, and sees the lowest balance she should expect over the next months.

## Subscriptions

A table of your subscriptions with the monthly and yearly cost and the next charge, the total per year, and a note when a price went up.

> 💡 **Real-life example** Kostas lists his subscriptions and finds he pays €38 a month for services he barely uses. He cancels two of them.

## Table

Every transaction in the period, in a table you can sort by clicking a heading and group by category, wallet or month, with totals. **CSV** downloads it for a spreadsheet.

Every chart has **Show as a table**, so the numbers are always available as text.

On a tablet or computer the charts and tiles use the extra room.
