Features

Everything about your money, in one app.

⚡

Add it in seconds

A big keypad, a category, done. Or type Coffee 3.50 yesterday or Netflix 15.99 monthly on the 3rd and Expenity fills the form.

👛

Wallets and currencies

Cash, bank, card and savings, each with its own currency. Totals are converted with daily rates and marked as approximate. Never "€10 + $5".

🎯

Budgets and goals

Set a limit for food, save for a trip, add your salary. See what is left, what you can spend per day, and when you will get there.

📈

Reports and a forecast

Spending by category, cash flow, trends, subscriptions, a sortable table, and the expected balance for the next 90 days.

💡

Ideas from your own data

"Still using Netflix? Cancelling saves €192 a year." Tell Expenity your aims and it points out where your money could go further.

👥

Shared wallets

Share a wallet with family or friends by invite code. Choose who can edit and who can only view. Everything else stays private.

How it works

From nothing to your first expense in five minutes.

Create an account with a passkey

Your fingerprint, face or device PIN. No password to remember. Or try it first without an account.

Add it to your home screen and set up

A short animation shows how. Then a few optional questions: your currency, what you want help with, your wallets, a look you like.

Add, plan and watch it add up

Add expenses and income, set a budget or a goal, and let Expenity suggest where you could save.

Plan

Save for something, and see when you will get there.

Name a goal, add money from a wallet, and watch the ring fill. Expenity estimates the date from how you have been saving, and tells you what a monthly amount would do. Budgets show what is left and how much you can spend each day.

Reports

Know where it goes, and where it is heading.

Pick a period, filter by wallet or category, and explore seven views, from a donut chart you can tap to a forecast of your balance. Every chart also comes as a table.

Themes

Make it yours. Or make it retro.

Twelve themes that change colour, shape and type: minimal, paper notes, ledger, retro terminal, synthwave, blueprint, pastel, glass and more. A little motion, and a switch to turn it off.

All twelve themes →

Shared wallets

One wallet, two people, no spreadsheet.

Invite someone with a code or a link. They can edit or only view, and the server enforces it. Each transaction shows who added it. Your other wallets, budgets and goals stay private.

An honest status

What works today, and what is next.

This is version {{version}}. It is complete enough to use every day, and still young: it has been tested in a real browser, not yet on every phone.

Works today

  • Passkey accounts, recovery codes and sync across devices, offline first
  • Expenses, income, transfers and bills, with a fast keypad and quick add
  • Wallets in several currencies with daily exchange rates
  • Budgets, goals, recurring income and ideas from your data
  • Reports: overview, spending, cash flow, trends, forecast, subscriptions, table
  • Shared wallets with roles, invite codes and links
  • Twelve themes, an animations switch, English and Greek
  • Calendar file with alarms, notifications while open, backup and CSV

Planned

  • Reminders by push and email when the app is closed (email needs a sending domain)
  • Email invites for shared wallets, and notifications
  • Reading a bill from a photo or a forwarded email
  • Telegram and SMS reminders
  • End-to-end encryption, as an option

Heads up: your data is stored on Expenity's server when you have an account, protected by your passkey but not end-to-end encrypted. Without an account it stays in your browser. Keep a backup either way: More → Data and backup.

Private by design

Your money is nobody else's business.

No password

You sign in with a passkey. The server stores only a public key.

Account optional

Try it with no account and your data stays in your browser.

No bank login

Expenity never asks for bank details and never moves money.

No ads, no tracking

Visits are counted anonymously, with no cookies and no personal data.

Take it with you

Download your data as JSON or CSV any time, or delete your account in one step.

Open source

The app and the server are public. Read the code, or run your own.

Why Expenity exists

Money apps are often stuck on one phone, want your bank login, or feel like a spreadsheet. Expenity started as a bill reminder and grew into what its author wanted to use: fast to add to, clean to look at, and honest about what it can and cannot do.

It is a web app, so it runs everywhere. It speaks Greek as well as English, because bills in Greece have their own words and dates. And it is open source, so you can see what it does with your data.
