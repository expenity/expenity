How it thinks

The question is never “do I have the money?”

It is “what will I have left after buying it, and is that enough for next month?” Here is the idea, with the car example everyone asks about.

  • 🏦 In your wallets+ €10,000
  • 🚗 The car you want− €9,000
  • 🛟 Left after buying€1,000
  • 🗓️ Your usual month€700
  • €1,000 is 1.4 months of your spending.
    With your salary on the way, next month stays positive. On track.

That is the whole trick. eFriend adds up what you have and what is coming, takes away the bills and the spending it has learned from your own history, and looks at what remains after the purchase.

  • 🛟It measures the leftover in months of your usual spending, because “€300” means nothing until you know what a month costs you.
  • 💶It checks your income too. €1,000 left is fine if a salary lands next week, and risky if it doesn’t.
  • 🔥It also checks whether you spend more than you earn. Then it measures how long the leftover lasts at your real monthly loss, so a plan never looks comfortable on a salary that does not cover your life.
  • 📈The longer you use Expenity, the sharper it gets. It tells you how many days of history it has, and how sure it is.

Everything it can do

Sixteen things, with the real screens.

Every picture below is a real screen from Expenity, with a made-up household called Maria. Nothing is mocked up.

1Plans, not savings goals

A plan is something to check, not a pot to fill. You give it a name, a cost, a date and the wallets it may use. You never have to “add money” to it. eFriend simply watches whether you will be able to afford it.

  • 🔗Optional: link a savings goal and eFriend counts what is already saved there.
  • 🔄Or turn an existing goal into a plan with Let eFriend manage this goal. Money already in it keeps counting.
  • 🤖Every goal also has an Ask eFriend button, to check it without changing it.
2A verdict you can read in two seconds

Each plan gets a face and one of five verdicts, Comfortable, On track, Tight, At risk or Unlikely. Tap Full analysis for the detail.

  • 💶Four tiles: cost, likely free, left after buying, days left.
  • 🛟How many months of spending that leftover covers.
  • 🧾Bills that cannot be skipped, with the reason each one cannot.
  • 🎚️How sure it is, and how many days of your history it has.
3Honest when you spend more than you earn

A verdict should never flatter you. If your spending is higher than your income, a one-month check is not enough: you are losing money every month. So eFriend looks at how long what is left would last at your real monthly loss, and says it plainly: “You spend about €1,200 a month more than you earn. What is left would last about 1.3 months.”

  • 🔥The monthly shortfall is shown in the analysis, with how long the leftover lasts.
  • 🟢A plan can only be Comfortable if what is left lasts 6 months or more and your cushion is intact.
  • 🟡On track needs 3 months or more. Tight needs 1 month. Less than that is At risk.
  • ✅If your income covers your spending, nothing changes.
4It sorts your spending: Must, Need, Want

Rent and your phone bill are not “skippable”. A streaming plan is. eFriend sorts every category for you using a bit of common sense, then asks you to correct it. Is a doctor a need or a want? Only you know. A three-way toggle changes it in a tap, and the analysis updates instantly.

  • 🔒Must: fixed and necessary.
  • 🛒Need: necessary, but you can shop smarter.
  • 🎁Want: nice to have.
5Budgets are its best friend

When a plan is off target, eFriend does not just worry. It proposes monthly budgets for the categories that cost you the most, tells you how much each could add by the date, and opens the budget form already filled in with Set this budget.

  • 🎯“A monthly Leisure budget of about €160 could add about €210.”
  • 🛑Two no-spend days a week, and the 24-hour rule for impulse wants.
  • ✂️A quarter off the biggest wants, or pausing a subscription, with the exact gain.
6Many wishes, one priority list

Give it as many plans as you like. When together they cost more than you will have, eFriend asks once: “Which one matters most?” You press and hold, then drag the plans into your order. The top one gets your money first.

  • 🏆“Trip to Paris is your top priority out of the 4.”
  • ✅Safe, fits, at risk, doesn’t fit, each plan placed honestly.
  • 💡“If you drop New car, Paris would fit.”
  • ✨Or tap Let eFriend suggest: cheapest first, so the most plans can happen.
7Your safety cushion, your risk

How much should still be left after you buy something? Half a month? Three? You decide with one slider. The track stays calm. Only the handle changes colour, blending smoothly from red through green to blue.

  • 🔴Risky: you are happy to spend almost everything.
  • 🟢Balanced: one month of your usual spending stays untouched.
  • 🔵Very safe: six months, a proper emergency fund.
8Your own limits, respected

“What is the most you are comfortable spending?” Set a maximum per day, per week and per month, in the tour or in your spending targets. eFriend plans around the lower of your limit and your real habits. And if your limits don’t line up (€40 a day is about €1,200 a month, but your monthly limit is €900) it tells you and uses the stricter one.

9The earliest safe date

For bigger amounts, the question is not only “can I?” but “when?”. eFriend looks up to a year ahead and finds the first date you could buy it safely. If it is further away than a year, it says so honestly.

  • 📅Suggest a date right inside the plan form.
  • 👆Use this date in the analysis, and the plan moves, with the change recorded.
  • 🔔Offered again on the day itself, if you cannot afford it yet.

You choose the pace. It respects it.

  • 🎚️Between 1 and 5 proposals can wait at once.
  • 📦Several goals? One combined card with the split inside, confirmed in one tap.

  • 🛡️It never goes below your safety cushion.
11Impossible to miss, easy to undo

Anything eFriend records stands out. A small robot badge, a coloured edge, the words Moved by eFriend, a one-tap Undo, and a weekly line: “This week eFriend moved €40.” On Home, your balance shows the total and how much of it is set aside for goals, so the numbers always add up.

12When the date arrives: “Did you make it?”

A full-screen question, because this deserves a moment. First eFriend tells you whether you can afford it today. Then you answer.

  • ✅Yes, I did it! It records the expense with an eFriend badge and a celebration, and adds it to What you achieved with eFriend.
  • 📅Not yet, move the date, with the earliest safe date already filled in.
  • 💔I changed my mind moves it to Past plans, and you can Reopen it any time.
13Reports that show the journey

A new eFriend tab in Reports draws how ready you were for each plan over time. Every edit you make, a new cost, a moved date, a changed sorting, becomes a numbered gold point on the line, with a plain sentence: “Cost changed from €3,900 to €3,300: closer to your plan (62% → 104% ready).”

  • 📉See how each decision moved you closer, or further.
  • 🧮Your weekly spending on wants, before your first plan and since. A fair comparison, never a claim.
  • 🏆A running total of what you made happen.
14A tour that feels like meeting someone

The first time, a full-screen tour introduces eFriend step by step. The logo’s E turns into an e, its tick into an F, and “riend” types itself out.

  • 💶Where does your money come from? Add your salary right there, and come back to the same step.
  • 💭What are you dreaming about? Tap a chip: a trip, shoes, a phone, a gift, a car.
  • 🎯Budgets, priorities, and how it can help move money, with fun little animations.
  • 🔁Missed it? Take the eFriend tour again, any time.
15A friend with a personality

The robot waves to get your attention, then waves with both hands. It blinks. Its face changes with the news. It has dozens of lines, some of them very silly (“To be or not to be… on budget?”), and they pass through its bubble one at a time, so the card never jumps around.

  • 😊Tap it and it smiles and waves.
  • 😆A few more taps and it giggles.
  • 😠Poke it too much and it shakes with a low growl. (Try it in the hero above!)
  • 🔊The sounds can be switched off with Robot sounds.

16Ask before you buy

Not everything needs a plan. Sometimes you just wonder: “Can I buy shoes that cost €49?” Type it the way you would say it. eFriend checks today, then looks at the next six weeks, your income dates, your bills, the way you usually spend, your own limits and your other plans, and finds the best day to spend that money.

  • 📅A calendar colours each day: Best, Good, Tight or Avoid. Paydays 💶 and bills 🧾 are marked, and ⭐ shows the first safe day.
  • 💶Often the best day is right after your salary lands and before the rent. Tap any day to see why.
  • 🪙Small purchase? It says so: a plan is optional.
  • 🎯Big one? Make it a plan for this date opens the plan form already filled in, so eFriend can keep watching.

    Private by design

    Smart does not have to mean watched.

    📱

    It runs on your device

    eFriend’s maths happens inside the app. There is no AI service behind it, no server call, no account. The same rules give the same answer every time.

    🙈

    Nobody sees your plans

    Your plans, amounts and proposals stay in your own data. Expenity does not collect usage statistics.

    Good questions

    Is eFriend free?

    It is part of the Advanced plan of Expenity, which is free. You can switch eFriend on or off in More → Features.

    Does it move my real money?

    Never. Expenity is a tracking system. When eFriend “moves” money to a goal, it only adds an entry in your records. If you also move the money in your bank, your notebook and your bank agree.

    Is it using AI?

    No. It is a set of clear rules that run on your device and use your own history. That is why it is private, fast and predictable.

    What if I have only used Expenity for a few days?

    It still answers, and it tells you plainly that it has little history and may be rough. It also gets sharper over time, and it asks for your salary if it does not know it.

    Can it drop a plan, or spend without asking?

    No. It only advises. In Automatic mode it records moves to the goals you allowed, within your safety cushion, with Undo, and only after you turn that mode on.

    Can I just ask it a question before buying something?

    Yes. Tap Ask on the Home card or open More → eFriend, type something like “Can I buy shoes that cost 49?”, and it answers for today and shows the best days on a calendar.

    Does it work in Greek?

    Yes, fully, with a formal, friendly tone. There is a Greek version of this page too.
