# 09 Plan for version 0.4: feedback on the beta

**Status:** draft for review, 2026-10-15. Nothing here is built yet.
**Source:** George's twelve points after trying the 0.3 beta on a phone. Each point is answered below with the root cause (where it was a mistake) and the fix.

## 1. The twelve points

| # | Feedback | Honest cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Onboarding is useless; a wallet can be created before registering | The first-run flow was bolted on at the front of an app that already worked without an account | Rebuild the flow in the right order (section 2) |
| 2 | Prompt to sign in or register on the loading screen unless already signed in | Guest mode made the app open straight to a dashboard | The first screen is **Sign in or create account** (section 2) |
| 3 | After registering, guide the user to add the app to the home screen, with an animation and a skip | Install prompt was a small card on Home, easy to miss | A dedicated **Install step** with an animated walkthrough per platform, skippable |
| 4 | A second onboarding (currency, wallets, income, name, theme, and so on), skippable, and only then the user's own data | Setup questions were two fields | A **Setup wizard** of several short steps, each skippable (section 2) |
| 5 | Reports are super basic | I shipped a minimum and called it done | A real **Reports** area: filters, pie and bar and line charts, tables, forecast, comparisons, export (section 4) |
| 6 | Where are budgets, goals, savings accounts? | I moved them to "phase 2" in the spec without asking you | Build **budgets, goals and savings** now (section 5) |
| 7 | Only two themes; want artistic, professional, retro, minimal, even paper notes | Light and dark only | A **theme system** with 12 themes, each with its own colors, shapes, type and decoration (section 6) |
| 8 | No animations; should be able to disable them | I stopped at "short and purposeful" in the spec and built almost none | A full **motion system** (transitions, charts, lists, numbers, celebrations) with an on/off setting that also follows the device's reduce-motion (section 7) |
| 9 | No sharing of wallets | Households were phase 4 | **Shared wallets** with invite links and roles (section 8). It needs server changes, so it is its own phase |
| 10 | The Greek is bad | I translated quickly and did not review it as a Greek user would | A full **Greek rewrite and review**, with a glossary, and a list of strings for you to correct (section 9) |
| 11 | Two currencies shown on overdue bills | A bill keeps the currency it was created with, while the base currency changed later in first run. Totals then print "€64 + $12" side by side | Fix the cause (bill currency follows its wallet), and show totals sensibly, never as "A + B" (section 3) |
| 12 | Tablet layout is poor, even scrolls sideways | I only tested phones, desktop and one tablet size, and horizontal scrolling rows (wallets, chips) are meant for phones | Tablet layouts with grids instead of side-scrolling rows, tested on real iPad sizes (section 10) |

## 2. The new flow (points 1 to 4)

```
Open the app
  signed in?  ──yes──▶ the app
  │ no
  ▼
 [1] Welcome: sign in or create account      (language picker on this screen)
  │   a quiet "Try without an account" link stays for people who want it (see decision D1)
  ▼
 [2] Add to home screen (after registering, before anything else)
  │   animated walkthrough for the platform, "Skip" and "Done"
  ▼
 [3] Setup wizard (each step skippable, progress dots, back button)
  │   name · currency · wallets · income · budget · goals · theme and motion
  ▼
 [4] The app, with an empty but welcoming Home
```

- **No data screens before step 4.** Wallets, transactions and bills appear only after the flow. Existing users with data skip it.
- **Resumable:** progress is saved. If the user quits halfway, the flow continues where it stopped.
- **Step 1, welcome:** the Expenity logo and one sentence, big buttons **Create account** and **Sign in**, language switch (EN, ΕΛ). Passkey explained in one line.
- **Step 2, install:** the animation shows the phone browser, the Share button, "Add to Home Screen", the app icon landing on the home screen. Separate animations for iPhone (Safari), Android (Chrome menu or the install button) and desktop. If the app is already installed, the step is skipped. "Skip" is always visible.
- **Step 3, setup wizard:**
  1. **What should we call you?** (display name, shown in the greeting)
  2. **Main currency** (suggested from the country; list with search)
  3. **Your wallets:** quick-add the usual ones (cash, bank account, card, savings) with opening balances; how many is up to the user
  4. **Income:** do you have a regular income? Amount, how often, which day (creates a recurring income)
  5. **Budget:** an optional monthly spending limit, or a suggested split
  6. **Goals:** an optional first savings goal
  7. **Look and feel:** theme (with live preview), accent color, animations on or off
  8. **Done:** a short summary and a celebration

## 3. Currency correctness (point 11)

- A **bill takes its wallet's currency**; changing the wallet changes the bill's currency (with a confirmation if amounts exist).
- The **base currency** is set once during setup, before any data exists. Changing it later asks what to do (keep wallets as they are; totals are shown per currency).
- **Totals never print as "€10 + $5".** Rules:
  - the main total is in the base currency, using exchange rates for other currencies (marked "about"), or
  - if rates are unavailable, other currencies are listed on their own lines.
- **Exchange rates:** daily rates from a public free source, fetched through our Worker (so the browser calls only our API), cached for 24 hours, and shown as approximate. Rates are labelled with their date.
- A one-time repair for existing local data: bills whose currency differs from their wallet's currency are offered a fix.

## 4. Reports (point 5)

A **Reports** area with a filter bar and tabs.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Period picker (this month, last month, 3 months, year to date, last 12 months, custom range). Net worth, income, expenses, savings rate, comparison with the previous period (arrows and percent) |
| **Spending** | Pie and donut by category with drill-down (tap a slice to see its transactions and subcategories), top merchants and descriptions, biggest expenses, average per day, heatmap by weekday |
| **Income and cash flow** | Income versus expenses by month (bars), cumulative cash flow (line), net per month, savings rate trend |
| **Trends** | Category trends over time (stacked areas), month-over-month changes, year-over-year when there is history |
| **Forecast** | Expected balance for the next 30, 60 and 90 days from recurring income, bills and typical spending, a line with an "if nothing changes" range, safe-to-spend today, upcoming big payments |
| **Subscriptions** | Table of subscriptions with monthly and yearly cost, next charge, price-change flags, total per year, cancel notes |
| **Tables** | Sortable table of transactions for the period with totals, group by category, wallet, month or label |

- **Filters:** wallets, categories, income or expense, amount range, labels, text. Filters apply across tabs and can be saved as named views.
- **Export:** CSV, JSON, a printable PDF-style page, and a copy of any chart as an image.
- Charts are drawn by the app (SVG), animated, accessible (each chart has a table equivalent) and follow the theme.

## 5. Budgets, goals and savings (point 6)

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Budgets** | Monthly (or weekly, yearly) limits per category or overall, with rollover as an option. Progress rings and bars, remaining amount, "safe to spend per day", alerts at 80 and 100 percent (in-app, and later by email or push), history of past months |
| **Goals** | A name, target amount, optional deadline, an icon and color. Contributions as transfers from a wallet, progress ring, projected completion date, milestones with a celebration |
| **Savings** | Savings wallets with a target and an optional interest rate. "Round-up" and "pay yourself first" rules (a recurring transfer to a goal). A **savings overview** on Home |
| **Recurring income** | Salary and other income as schedules that create expected entries, confirmed with one tap |
| **Planned vs actual** | A monthly plan (expected income, bills, budgets) against what actually happened |

All of these are new record kinds in the existing sync (budgets and goals are already accepted by the server).

## 6. Themes (point 7)

A theme is more than a color: it sets palette, shapes, type, borders, shadows, icon style, backgrounds and small decorations. Each theme has light and dark behavior where it makes sense, and an accent color choice. A live preview is shown in setup and in **More, Appearance**.

| Theme | Character |
|---|---|
| **Emerald** (current) | Calm fintech green, soft cards |
| **Minimal** | Monochrome, hairlines, no shadows, lots of white space, tiny type accents |
| **Paper notes** | Cream paper, ruled lines, handwritten-style headings, sticky-note cards, tape, slight tilts |
| **Ledger** | Accounting book: serif type, double rules, columns, green-tinted paper |
| **Retro terminal** | Green or amber on black, monospace, scanline touch, blinking cursor |
| **80s synthwave** | Dark purple, neon pink and cyan gradients, glow, a grid horizon |
| **Blueprint** | Blue drawing paper, white line art, dashed borders, grid background |
| **Pastel candy** | Soft pastels, large rounded shapes, playful emoji-like accents |
| **Midnight OLED** | True black, thin color accents, high contrast |
| **Glass** | Frosted translucent cards over a soft gradient |
| **Sunset** | Warm gradients, oranges and corals |
| **Notebook (dark)** | Dark chalkboard with chalk-like type |

Fonts use the system stacks (no external font service), with handwriting and monospace fallbacks per platform. Themes are checked for contrast and for every screen and sheet. Themes can be added later through a small configuration object.

## 7. Motion (point 8)

- **Where:** tab changes, list items appearing, sheets (slide and spring), swipe feedback, number count-ups, chart drawing (bars grow, rings fill, lines draw), progress and goal rings, success checkmark and confetti on goals, empty-state illustrations, onboarding animations (including the install walkthrough), pull-to-refresh, skeleton loading.
- **Settings:** **More, Appearance, Animations:** Full, Reduced, Off. The default follows the device's "reduce motion" preference. "Off" disables everything including transitions.
- **Performance:** only transform and opacity animations, short durations, nothing blocks input.

## 8. Shared wallets (point 9)

Sharing is a server feature, so it is designed separately.

- **Model:** a wallet can be **shared** with other Expenity accounts. Members see the wallet's transactions, and the budgets, goals and bills attached to it. Roles: **Owner**, **Editor** (add and change), **Viewer** (read only).
- **Invites without email:** the owner creates an **invite link or code** (expires, single use, role chosen). The other person opens it, signs in, and joins. Email invites come when a sending domain exists (spec 07, risk R3).
- **Sync design:** records belong to a **space** (a personal space, or a shared wallet space). Each user syncs their personal space plus every space they belong to. The server checks the member's role on every write. Last-writer-wins stays per record. Removing a member stops syncing for them and removes the space from their device.
- **Privacy:** the interface shows who can see a wallet and who added each transaction. Members never see personal wallets.
- **Activity feed** in shared wallets (who added or changed what), and optional notifications later.

## 9. Greek (point 10)

- Full rewrite of all strings by meaning, not word for word, using natural modern Greek and consistent finance terms.
- **Glossary first** (examples to agree with you): wallet = Πορτοφόλι, expense = Έξοδο, income = Έσοδο, bill = Λογαριασμός (or Πληρωμή; "λογαριασμός" also means bank account, so wallets use Πορτοφόλι and bills use Λογαριασμοί/Πληρωμές), budget = Προϋπολογισμός, goal = Στόχος, recurring = Επαναλαμβανόμενο, transfer = Μεταφορά, passkey, recovery code = Κωδικός ανάκτησης.
- Short labels where space is tight (tab bar, chips), formal register but friendly (εσείς), correct plurals and grammar for amounts and dates.
- The same review for the website, help center and blog post.
- Review loop: I produce a **Greek review sheet** (every string with its context) and you mark what to change. Only a native speaker can make this right; my pass is a strong first version, not a final one.

## 10. Tablet, and layout quality (point 12)

- On widths of 600 px and up, **horizontal scrolling rows become grids or wrapping rows** (wallet cards, category chips, filters).
- Two-column and three-column compositions for Home, Reports and Settings; the details pane appears at 900 px.
- **Test matrix:** iPad mini portrait (744x1133), iPad Air (820x1180), iPad Pro 11 (834x1194) and 13 (1024x1366) in both orientations, Android tablets (800x1280, 1280x800), laptops (1280x800, 1440x900), and the phone sizes. Automatic checks for horizontal scroll, text overlap, clipped content and tap-target size on every screen at every size; screenshot review before showing you.
- Landscape phone is supported (rail appears at 600 px).
