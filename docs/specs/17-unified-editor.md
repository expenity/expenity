# Unified editor and optional audio — v0.16.0

Expense, upcoming bill and recurring income use the same composer. Amount and name stay prominent. Wallet, date, category and repeat use readable rows; tags, notes, photos and bill metadata live under More details. Existing records hide sentence-entry shortcuts and service suggestions. Inputs do not open the keyboard until selected.

Repeat opens a focused page within the same sheet. Back preserves the draft. Frequency choices reveal relevant controls; ending rules and upcoming dates are expandable. Missing-day choices appear only for dates that can be absent in shorter months. Saving an existing schedule updates that record without posting a transaction or restarting an unchanged schedule.

A scan launched by holding + owns its new expense draft. Closing the scanner cancels both. Scanning inside a manual draft retains that draft when cancelled. Recognition success retains the editor for review.

Appearance offers exactly two sound profiles: Minimal and Normal. Off by default, with volume and explicit preview. Short locally synthesized cues cover saving, payments, undo, errors and scan readiness; no remote sound files or per-use costs.

Validation: targeted browser coverage of bill/income editing, ordinary expense saving, scan cancellation, sound preferences, English/Greek layouts at phone/tablet/desktop widths, and service-worker release updates. Public website, published documentation and blog remain unchanged.
