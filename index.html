01 / EXPENITY

Less friction.
More perspective.

One expense becomes a pattern. A pattern becomes a decision. And somewhere between the two, Expenity quietly does the boring parts for you. Here is just how packed it is.

Split the payment. Keep the detail.

€60 spent, €40 on groceries and €20 on shopping. One wallet payment, accurate category budgets. Available in Advanced.

See the example

Know what is still owed.

Track full or partial repayments without counting them as earnings. See the remaining amount and a clear receipt history. Available in Advanced.

Record it. Keep moving.

Tap the amount, confirm, done. Save a name once and reuse it. Or just type a sentence like “Coffee 3.50 yesterday” and watch it fill itself in.

Scan a receipt.

Hold + and point your camera. The merchant, total and date are read on your device, in English and Greek. Not quite right? Re-scan.

A number for today.

One friendly figure: what you can spend today. Keep the automatic one, or set your own daily, weekly or monthly target. Add an income target too.

Keep the flame going.

Streaks count the days you stay within budget, with your best run and your no-spend days. Quietly addictive, in a good way.

Give your goals a home.

Budgets and savings goals with rings and bars that fill as you go. Gentle warnings arrive before the limit, not after.

Make payday predictable.

Salary, rent and subscriptions repeat on their own schedule. Income that is already due is recorded as received straight away.

See the whole picture.

Charts, tables, filters and a 90-day forecast. Explore what happened and what may be coming.

Catch the little slips.

The duplicate guard asks before you add the same entry twice, and price-change alerts flag a bill that quietly got more expensive.

Find it. Fix it. Finish it.

Filter by amount, dates and tags. Restore deleted items for 30 days. Unfinished entries are kept as drafts and waiting on Home.

A photo, not a pile of paperwork.

Attach a small photo to an entry. Pick a profile picture from 20 characters (funny, animated, minimal) or use your own.

Share the everyday.

Invite family or friends to one wallet with viewing or editing rights. Your other wallets stay your own.

Learn as you go.

Twenty-five animated tutorials and a pulsing help icon that explains the screen you are on, in a popup, without losing your place.

Only what you need.

Choose Basic, Pro or Advanced, or switch features on and off one by one. Nothing is deleted when you hide something.

Make it look like you.

Eighteen themes, and a pencil on each one to adjust colours, font and roundness. Bundled fonts look right on every device.

Say hello.

A contact form with formatting and photos sits inside the app, for feedback or a problem report. No account needed.

WORKS WHERE YOU DO

One app. Every screen you own.

Expenity is a web app that installs like a real one, so there is nothing to download from a store and nothing to update by hand. It works offline, adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and your data follows you when you sign in.

iPhone and iPad

Open Expenity in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like any other app.

Good to know: Notifications when the app is closed need the home-screen version.

Android

Open it in Chrome and choose Install app. It gets its own icon and works offline.

Good to know: Receipt scanning uses your camera, or a photo from your gallery.

Windows, Mac and Linux

Use it right in the browser, or install it from Chrome or Edge (the install icon in the address bar), or add it to the Dock from Safari on a Mac.

Good to know: Firefox works in the browser but cannot install web apps on a computer.

03 / YOUR WAY

As much app as you want.

Packed does not mean cluttered. Pick a plan on day one and change it any time. Hidden features come back exactly as you left them.

Basic

Wallets, expenses, income, bills, categories and reports. Simple and quiet.

Pro

Adds repeating bills and income, budgets and goals, tags, photos, shared wallets, notifications, the daily number, filters and more.

Advanced

Everything: receipt scanning, ideas and forecasts, streaks, sounds, Home editing and theme editing.

Your account. Clear choices.

Financial records work offline. Sign in to sync across devices. Photos need an account and internet. Account data is stored on Cloudflare over an encrypted connection; it is not end-to-end encrypted. No bank connection is required.

Security, checked.

Before sharing Expenity we reviewed the app and its server from end to end. There are no secrets in the code or its history, the dependencies are audited with no known vulnerabilities, and the server has rate limits, strict input checks, security headers and safeguards around push and email. We also tested the app with hostile text in every place you can type, so it can only ever be shown as plain text. Sign-in uses passkeys, so there is no password to steal. This was our own thorough review, not an independent audit, and no software is ever perfectly safe. If you find a problem, please tell us through Contact us in the app.

04 / WHY IT EXISTS

Made for Olga.

Expenity started with a very simple request: Olga wanted an easy way to track expenses.

That one word, easy, became the brief. Every feature here is an answer to “could that be easier?”, from the sentence you can type to the help icon that waits patiently on every screen. It is free, open source and made for every screen, in English and Greek.
