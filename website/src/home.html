01 / EXPENITY

Less friction.
More perspective.

One expense becomes a pattern. A pattern becomes a decision. Expenity connects the small things you record with the bigger things you want.

Record it. Keep moving.

Tap the amount to open the keypad. Confirm to put it away. Save a name once and reuse it for your next expense or income.

Give your goals a home.

Budgets & goals brings together spending limits and savings targets. A gentle discovery dot shows you where to start.

Make payday predictable.

Activity → Income → Recurring income. Set up salary or regular payments, then mark them received when they arrive.

See the whole picture.

Charts, tables, filters and a 90-day forecast. Explore what happened and what may be coming.

A photo, not a pile of paperwork.

Add a small reference image to an expense, bill, wallet or goal. Compressed before upload. Manage your photos from Account.

Share the everyday.

Invite family or friends to one wallet. Choose viewing or editing rights while keeping your other wallets separate.
