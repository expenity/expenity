How it works

Three bills in two minutes.

Say what you pay

Type a sentence, pick a template, or fill in the form. Expenity understands amounts, dates and “monthly” or “every Friday”.

Netflix15.99 EURMonthlyTue 3 Nov

Get reminded

Choose how early you want to hear about it, from the day itself up to two weeks before. Add everything to your calendar with one download.

Mark it paid

One tap. The next due date is set automatically, and your payment history builds up for the insights.

The calendar

Your reminders, in the calendar you already use.

A website cannot wake your phone when it is closed. Your calendar can. So Expenity gives you one file with every bill, its repeat rule and an alarm, and your Google, Apple or Outlook calendar does the reminding on every device.

  • Alarms at 09:00 on the reminder day and on the due day.
  • Repeating bills repeat in the calendar too.
  • Payment reference and link are in the event notes.

Insights

Know what is coming before it comes.

Week-by-week bars for the next 13 weeks, spending by category, the yearly cost of every subscription, and a heads-up when something got more expensive.

Why Expenity exists

Bill reminder apps are useful and often stuck on one phone: lose it and you lose your list, and your partner never sees it. Many want an account, or your bank login, before they help.

Expenity starts from the opposite end. It is a web app, so it runs everywhere. It needs no account to be useful. It speaks Greek as well as English, because bills in Greece have their own words and dates. And it is honest about the hard part: a closed website cannot wake your phone, so the reminders you can rely on today live in your calendar.
