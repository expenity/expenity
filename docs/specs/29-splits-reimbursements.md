# Split expenses and reimbursements — Advanced

One expense remains one wallet payment, with optional category allocations stored inside the transaction. Allocations must be positive integer-cent amounts, at least two rows, and sum exactly to the parent amount. Attachments, tags, date and wallet belong to the payment. Category reports and budgets expand allocations virtually; balances and activity count the payment once.

An expense may identify a person or organisation expected to repay part or all of it. Expected amount cannot exceed the payment. Repayments are separate cash-income records linked by reimbursementOf, in the same currency and sharing scope. They retain category allocation snapshots, so deleting an original expense does not silently turn repayments into earnings. Multiple partial repayments are allowed, up to the locally known expected amount. Concurrent offline receipts are retained and over-collection is explicitly flagged for review. Reports treat receipts as negative expenses on the receipt date, not salary. Expected but unpaid money never increases balances or safe-to-spend. Removing a receipt reopens the amount due. Editing originals cannot reduce expected amounts below receipts already recorded. Existing repayment links cannot be converted into ordinary income or scheduled entries. Concurrent edits use existing record stamps/LWW; server atomic multi-record transactions are outside the current sync protocol.

Both controls are Advanced (or explicitly enabled in Custom). Existing accounting and details remain correct when controls are disabled. Examples are explicit unsaved drafts, never automatically created transactions. New tutorials appear in Advanced onboarding and Help. Full EN/EL help, examples, feature matrix, release notes, and website tutorial export accompany release. Greek terminology: «Κατανομή εξόδου», «Επιστροφή χρημάτων από τρίτους», «Αναμενόμενο ποσό», «Εισπράχθηκαν», «Υπόλοιπο προς είσπραξη». Avoid confusing reimbursement with a merchant refund or salary.

Acceptance: exact cents, split reports/budgets, repayment caps and currency/scope, edit/duplicate/delete/undo, retained records with feature off, offline and sync round-trip, bilingual layouts/motion/keyboard, examples and tutorials.
