    Getting started

    Duenest keeps your bills and subscriptions in one place and tells you what is coming up. This guide takes about two minutes.

    Open Duenest#

    Open the app at george-stathopoulos.github.io/duenest/app/. There is nothing to install and no account to create. It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. To keep it on your home screen like an app, see Install on your device.

    Add your first bill#

    Press Add bill. You have three ways to start:

    • Type a sentence, for example Netflix 15.99 monthly on the 3rd. The form below fills itself and the chips show what Duenest understood. More examples.
    • Pick a template such as Rent, Electricity or Car insurance. It sets the name, category and how often it repeats. You add the amount.
    • Fill in the form yourself.

    Check the form and press Save bill. The bill appears on the Today screen.

    If you want to look around first, press Load sample bills on the empty screen. You can delete them later with Settings → Delete all my data.

    The Today screen#

    Today shows three totals (overdue, the next 7 days, the rest of the month) and your bills grouped by when they are due. Overdue bills are red and come first.

    On each bill:

    • Paid records the payment and moves a repeating bill to its next date. Mark paid.
    • ⋯ opens snooze, skip, payment link, copy reference, edit and delete.
    • Tapping the bill opens it for editing.

    Get reminded#

    Duenest reminds you in two ways, and it is worth setting up the second one: see Reminders.
