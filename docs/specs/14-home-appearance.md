# Home appearance editing

Home editing includes an Appearance button alongside Cards, Cancel and Done. Existing touch-and-hold entry and the Settings fallback remain unchanged.

Appearance edits the active template without switching to Custom or losing its scenery. Controls: page, surface, accent, text and icon colors; thirteen font choices (the three original families plus ten bundled fonts); square, slightly rounded, soft and extra-rounded corners. Readability checks block unreadable text/icon combinations.

The sheet previews colors and typography. Preview on Home stages changes in the Home editing draft. Done commits both appearance and layout to synced settings; Cancel discards both. Closing the appearance sheet without applying changes leaves the draft unchanged. Reset theme styling clears the active template override, independently of layout. Other templates keep their own overrides.

Ten local fonts: Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Roboto, Roboto Slab, Open Sans, Fira Sans, Comfortaa, Alegreya, Literata, Geologica. Greek and Latin subsets and regular/bold weights are bundled and cached offline; fonts make no runtime external requests. License files accompany the fonts.

Targeted checks cover draft cancellation, template retention, saved settings/reload, font loading, independent reset and EN/EL editor overflow at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
