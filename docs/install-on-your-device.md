# Install on your device

Expenity is a web app. You can use it in a browser tab, or install it so it opens in its own window with its own icon, and works offline.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open Expenity in **Safari**.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## Android

1. Open Expenity in **Chrome**.
2. Open the menu (⋮).
3. Choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

## Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose **Install Expenity**. Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock.

## Offline

After the first visit the app files are stored on your device, so Expenity opens and works without a connection. Your bills are stored on the device too. If a new version is available it updates the next time you open it with a connection.

## One device at a time

Your data lives in the browser on the device you use. A phone and a laptop do not share bills yet. Until sync exists, pick one main device, and use the calendar file for reminders on others: see [Reminders](reminders.md). You can move your bills between devices with a backup: see [Privacy and your data](privacy-and-your-data.md#back-up-and-restore).
